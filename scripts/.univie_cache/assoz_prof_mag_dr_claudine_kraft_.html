CV NEUE PROFESSUREN IM Jänner 2015

Assoz. Prof. Mag. Dr. Claudine Kraft

Assoziierte Professur am Zentrum für Molekulare Biologie

Curriculum Vitae:

born 1978 in Basel, Switzerland
1997-2001 Diploma at the University of Basel, Switzerland, Diploma program in Molecular Biology
2000-2001 Diploma thesis in Biochemistry at the University of Manchester, UK: "A molecular analysis of the ER components associated with a membrane inserted fragment of the amyloid precursor protein" with Prof. Steven High
2001-2005 Ph.D. (Dr.rer.nat.) in Molecular Biology at the University of Vienna, PhD thesis: "Regulation and substrate recognition of the anaphase-promoting complex in mitosis" with Dr. Jan-Michael Peters, Research Institute of Molecular Pathology (IMP), Vienna, Austria
2006-2011 Postdoctoral researcher, ETH Zürich, Switzerland:  "Selectivity and regulation of autophagy in yeast" with Prof. Matthias Peter (this time includes two maternity leaves)
2011-2014 Assistant Professor (tenure-track) at the Max F. Perutz Laboratories, University of Vienna, WWTF Vienna Research Group Leader award: "Regulation and signaling in autophagy"
since January 2015 Associate Professor, Department of Biochemistry and Cell Biology, University of Vienna

Research Areas:

* Autophagy
* Molecular Biology
* Biochemistry
* Cell Biology
* Genetics

Links:

  • Department of Biochemistry and Cell Biology, Centre for Molecular Biology
  • Max F. Perutz Laboratories (MFPL)
