CV NEUE PROFESSUREN IM Januar 2015

Univ.-Prof. Dipl.-Biol. Dr. Sascha Martens, Privatdoz.

Professur für Membranbiochemie am Zentrum für Molekulare Biologie

Curriculum Vitae:

2000 Diplombiologe, University of Cologne
2001 "Klaus-Liebrecht-Prize" for one of the two best Master or PhD studies at the Mathematical and Scientific Faculty of the University of Cologne, Germany
2004 Doctoral degree (Dr. rer. nat.) in Genetics, Institute for Genetics, University of Cologne, Germany
2004-2005 Postdoc, Institute for Genetics of the University of Cologne, Germany
2006 EMBO Long-Term Fellowship Award
2005-2009 Postdoc and Permanent Investigator Scientist, MRC Laboratory of Molecular Biology in Cambridge, UK
2007 AAAS/Science Program for Excellence in Science Award
2009-2014 Group leader/Assistant Professor, MFPL, University of Vienna, Austria
2010 ERC Starting Grant Award 2010
2011 Member of the "Junge Kurie" of the Austrian Academy of Sciences
2013 EMBO Young Investigator Award
2015-2017 Associate Professor, Department of Biochemistry and Cell Biology, University of Vienna
since September 2017 Professor, Department of Biochemistry and Cell Biology, University of Vienna 

Research Areas:

* Biochemistry
* Cell biology
* Membrane Biology
* Autophagy

Links:

  • Department of Biochemistry and Cell Biology, Centre for Molecular Biology
  • Max F. Perutz Laboratories (MFPL)
